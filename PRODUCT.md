# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary users are course professors, potential partners, partner institutions, publishers, research organizations, education collaborators, and funders who need to quickly understand Neu-reality's credibility, mission, network, and collaboration potential.

They arrive in an evaluative context: they are deciding whether Neu-reality is a serious, trustworthy, intellectually capable organization worth partnering with. The site should help them understand the platform's scientific focus, public influence, contributor network, education initiatives, and cross-sector collaborations without feeling like a conventional media kit.

Secondary users include students, researchers, experts, authors, podcast guests, event participants, and readers who want to discover Neu-reality's articles, interviews, podcasts, activities, and Noetex Academy programs.

## Product Purpose

Neu-reality is a non-profit science communication platform dedicated to the frontiers of brain science, intelligence research, neuroscience, artificial intelligence, neurotechnology, clinical applications, and industry trends. It promotes public understanding of science and encourages interdisciplinary dialogue across academia, technology, education, and publishing.

This website introduces Neu-reality to partners and institutions. It should communicate:

- What Neu-reality is and why it matters.
- Its long-term vision for brain science, intelligence, and the future of human knowledge.
- Its achievements, including a subscriber base exceeding one million and nearly 400 global academic contributors.
- Its content ecosystem: articles, interviews, podcasts, activities, newsletters, and industry services.
- Its education platform, Noetex Academy, and its mentor-led interdisciplinary learning model.
- Its special topics such as neuroaesthetics.
- Its collaboration network across universities, publishers, research institutes, technology companies, venture funds, and non-profit organizations.
- Its partner and platform links.

The public-facing site can use mock data for each content section at first, but it must include a clear admin entry that allows the site owner to import structured data for each section directly.

## Brand Personality

Neu-reality should feel calm, warm, contemporary, intellectually serious, and lightly experimental. It should carry a sense of frontier science and future inquiry, but technology should not overpower the human, educational, and cultural dimensions of the platform.

The tone should be credible and reflective rather than promotional. It should feel suitable for professors, institutional partners, and education collaborators, while still suggesting curiosity, imagination, and openness to emerging forms of science communication.

Suggested personality words:

- Grounded
- Warm
- Exploratory
- Scholarly
- Future-facing

## Anti-references

The site should not look like:

- A generic SaaS landing page with oversized hero metrics and identical card grids.
- A cold AI startup site dominated by dark blue, neon gradients, glowing orbs, or cyber aesthetics.
- A corporate brochure that feels bureaucratic, stiff, or overly institutional.
- A media portal that buries the mission beneath endless content feeds.
- A course-sales page that makes Noetex Academy feel transactional rather than intellectually formative.

Avoid designs where "technology" becomes the only visual signal. Brain science, education, public knowledge, long-term inquiry, and cross-cultural collaboration should be equally present.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with institutional trust, not spectacle.
   The first impression should make professors and partner institutions feel that Neu-reality is serious, established, and worth a deeper conversation.

2. Make complexity legible.
   Neu-reality spans science communication, contributors, events, podcasts, education, industry services, and partnerships. The site should organize this ecosystem clearly without flattening it into a generic grid.

3. Keep the frontier human.
   Neuroscience and AI are future-facing, but the design should foreground people, ideas, learning, mentorship, dialogue, and public understanding.

4. Let achievements support the story.
   Subscriber numbers, contributors, rankings, events, and playback counts should reinforce credibility, but they should not become the entire identity.

5. Design for future content operations.
   Every major section should be structured so mock content can later be replaced through imported data from an admin entry.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site should aim for WCAG AA-level contrast, readable typography, clear keyboard navigation, and responsive layouts across desktop, tablet, and mobile.

Motion should be purposeful and optional where possible. The vision section may use a video background, but text contrast and readability must remain reliable, and reduced-motion users should receive a stable fallback.

Because the site speaks to international academic and institutional audiences, layout and components should tolerate bilingual or longer English and Chinese content without text overflow.

6. Featured Podcasts

9. Neuroaesthetics Topic, combining articles, podcasts, and events

## Key Facts

- Neu-reality focuses on frontier science communication around brain science, artificial intelligence, neuroscience, neurotechnology, clinical applications, and industry trends.
- It has attracted more than one million subscribers across major platforms.
- It has gathered nearly 400 academic contributors from institutions such as MIT, Harvard, Stanford, Oxford, Cambridge, Tsinghua University, Peking University, the University of Hong Kong, Max Planck Institutes, and the Institute of Neuroscience, Chinese Academy of Sciences.
- Its cooperation network includes organizations such as Alibaba, Ant Group, Renmin University of China, Berggruen Institute, ZhenFund, Lanchi Ventures, CITIC Press, publishers, research institutes, technology companies, and non-profit organizations.
- Mind+ public events in Beijing and Shanghai have covered autism, schizophrenia, decision-making, neuroaesthetics, and related topics, with live and replay views approaching 500,000.
- Neuromancing features conversations with scientists, physicians, and philosophers and has exceeded one million plays on Ximalaya alone.
- NeuroFrontier delivers research insights, practical skills, and weekly paper digests to the academic community.
- NeuS tracks advances in neuroscience-related industries and encourages collaboration between academia and industry.
- Noetex Academy is a global future education platform incubated by Neu-reality. It offers interdisciplinary learning across neuroscience, cognitive science, artificial intelligence, life sciences, and social sciences through study programs, university visits, international conferences, research projects, and community co-creation.

## Naming Note

Neu-reality carries a dual meaning: reality is presented through neural activity, and neuroscience and neurotechnology may lead humanity toward a new reality.

Neu-reality is an independent organization headquartered in Shanghai, China, and is not affiliated with the Israeli company NeuReality.
